# Touch 3 — Day 10 — pure value, no pitch

No CTA. None. The absence is the point.

```
{first_name},

Not pitching anything here, just saw this and thought of {company}.

{one or two sentences: a regulatory change in their market, a data point about
their category, a competitor move they would want to know about}

{optional: link to the source}

{sender.first_name}
```

If you cannot find something actually useful, skip this touch rather than
manufacturing it. A fake value email is worse than silence because it is
transparently a pretext.
