# Touch 2 — Day 3 — proof

Under 60 words. One specific result with a number in it. A link is allowed here.

```
{first_name},

Sent a note a few days ago about {the thing}.

Quick proof point: {comparable_company} {did the thing} in {timeframe} and
{measurable outcome}. {one clause on what they did not have to do themselves}.

Still worth 15 minutes?

{sender.first_name}
```

Never invent a customer result. If `proof_points` in config is empty, use a
sourced market fact and cite it. A fabricated proof point is the fastest way to
lose a deal you were winning, and every industry is smaller than it looks.
