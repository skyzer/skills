# Touch 5 — Day 28 — the break-up

No CTA. Honest, no guilt. Concrete beats vague.

```
{first_name},

Looks like the timing is off, no problem.

If {the thing you do} ever becomes relevant for {company}, here is what day one
looks like: {concrete, sequential, three clauses, ending in a timeframe}.

No pressure either way. Wishing {company} well.

{sender.first_name}
```

"You'd drop in the widget, we handle compliance, your users are transacting
within 48 hours" gives them something to remember in six months.
"Let me know if anything changes" does not.
